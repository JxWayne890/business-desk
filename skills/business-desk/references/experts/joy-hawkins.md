### Avoid selective review gating

Original research status: candidate.

Use a review request process consistent with current platform rules and client permission. Do not withhold public review options based on customer satisfaction.

* [Google Review Gating - What Is Google Review Gating Policy? - Risks of Review Gating Google](https://www.sterlingsky.ca/review-gating-is-now-against-the-google-my-business-guidelines/). Author: Joy Hawkins. Locator: What Is Google Review Gating?. Do not selectively route satisfied customers to public reviews while withholding that option from others. Verify current Google policies and obtain permission for testimonials shown on the website.
