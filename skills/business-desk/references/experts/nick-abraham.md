## Methods

### Combine outcome, proof and reasonable buyer risk

Original research status: supported.

Offer only what the business can deliver; relevant proof matters more than decorative copy.

* [Simple valuable outreach](https://x.com/NickAbraham12/status/2000580686248321287). Author: Nick Abraham. Locator: Post text. Combine a concise message, credible proof, a bounded offer and an easy response.
* [Components of an offer](https://x.com/NickAbraham12/status/1740447915917582376). Author: Nick Abraham. Locator: Post text. Explain the desired outcome, real proof and any legitimate reduction in buyer risk.
* [Proof relevant to the buyer](https://x.com/NickAbraham12/status/2106020177921872313). Author: Nick Abraham. Locator: Post text. Comparable customer evidence and real warranty terms can make a modest offer understandable.

### Do not overinterpret small copy tests

Original research status: candidate.

Treat sparse positive replies as directional; prefer material hypotheses.

* [Choose material tests](https://x.com/NickAbraham12/status/2104570867715838426). Author: Nick Abraham. Locator: Post text. Do not crown a variant from one or two replies; investigate audience, offer and angle.

### Isolate campaign variables in delivery tests

Original research status: candidate.

Use controlled authorized test recipients and preserve uncertainty about real inbox placement.

* [Controlled delivery diagnosis](https://x.com/NickAbraham12/status/2106444972622831648). Author: Nick Abraham. Locator: Post text. Compare an ordinary control and campaign copy through the same authorized test setup.
