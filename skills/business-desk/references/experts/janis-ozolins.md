## Methods

### Make each element serve the idea

Original research status: supported.

Choose what the reader should understand. Remove decorative elements that compete with that understanding. A personal photograph may serve context or emotion instead.

* [Thread remove](https://x.com/OzolinsJanis/status/1392436333281521666). Author: Janis Ozolins. Locator: Author text in post 1392436333281521666. Avoid unnecessary cleverness, overexplaining, and elements that do not serve the idea.
* [Make Average Ideas Look Extraordinary](https://ozo.art/archive/make-average-ideas-look-extraordinary/). Author: Janis Ozolins. Locator: Opening and novelty examples. A visual can contribute a fresh way to understand a familiar idea through metaphor or a useful change to a familiar format. Decoration alone is a different purpose.

### Explore the idea in writing before drawing

Original research status: supported.

Write observations, feelings, comparisons, possible evidence, and a takeaway. Sketch after the idea is clear.

* [Thread notes](https://x.com/OzolinsJanis/status/1392436334288064514). Author: Janis Ozolins. Locator: Author text in post 1392436334288064514. Collect observations, feelings, and visual objects before drawing.
* [Overlooked Step Before Explaining Ideas Visually](https://ozo.art/archive/overlooked-step-before-explaining-ideas-visually/). Author: Janis Ozolins. Locator: Opening and four brainstorming areas. Write notes before sketching. Explore feelings, comparisons, data, and a takeaway. Verify any numbers before making a chart.

### Choose the intended reader reaction

Original research status: supported.

Aim for a reaction that belongs with the post, such as recognition, understanding, or hope. Do not manufacture the owner’s personal experience.

* [Thread feeling](https://x.com/OzolinsJanis/status/1392436331532402692). Author: Janis Ozolins. Locator: Author text in post 1392436331532402692. Plan how the reader should feel, then communicate clearly.
* [Want engagement Evoke emotional reaction](https://ozo.art/archive/want-engagement-evoke-emotional-reaction/). Author: Janis Ozolins. Locator: Specific reactions. Plan the intended reader reaction before styling. Relatability and understanding are useful goals. The named reaction vocabulary partly credits Shaan Puri. Do not treat memory claims as independently established science.

### Show the difference that matters

Original research status: supported.

Choose a meaningful comparison in time, expectations, process, or alternatives. Use truthful labels and data.

* [Overlooked Step Before Explaining Ideas Visually](https://ozo.art/archive/overlooked-step-before-explaining-ideas-visually/). Author: Janis Ozolins. Locator: Opening and four brainstorming areas. Write notes before sketching. Explore feelings, comparisons, data, and a takeaway. Verify any numbers before making a chart.
* [How to Make Your Visuals Pop with Smarter Comparisons](https://ozo.art/archive/how-to-make-your-visuals-pop-with-smarter-comparisons/). Author: Janis Ozolins. Locator: Closing advice and comparison examples. Choose the difference the reader needs to see. Comparisons can show time, expectations, process, and different ways of thinking, beyond good and bad.

### Keep one central idea in one explanatory visual

Original research status: candidate.

Prefer a single message for a feed graphic. Complex material may need a sequence or a separate document.

* [One Visual One Idea](https://ozo.art/archive/one-visual-one-idea). Author: Janis Ozolins. Locator: Opening and Simplicity is hard. Choose a central message and remove competing ideas. The author’s claimed seconds of attention are a heuristic, not a platform measurement.

### Use size and weight to guide reading order

Original research status: candidate.

Emphasize the main idea and subordinate labels. Choose final sizes by inspecting the image at phone width.

* [Size and weight guide the reading order](https://ozo.art/archive/4-ideas-life-happens-honor-freedom-loop-till-u-know-size-matters/). Author: Janis Ozolins. Locator: Size matters. Use type size and weight to create a reading order. This supports hierarchy, not an exact social font size.
