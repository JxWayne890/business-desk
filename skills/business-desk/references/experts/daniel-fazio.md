## Methods

### Explain the value behind the deliverable

Original research status: supported.

Translate the actual service into a buyer outcome without changing the business to lead generation.

* [Outcomes rather than a bundle](https://www.clientascension.io/blogs/if-i-restarted-my-10m-agency-today-this-is-the-only-offer-i-d-sell). Author: Daniel Fazio. Locator: The One Rule section. Frame a lead generation offer around a result, rather than its component tasks.
* [Explain outcomes behind deliverables](https://x.com/danielfazio/status/1954908998152388760). Author: Daniel Fazio. Locator: Post text. Make the customer result intelligible rather than listing implementation units.

### Capability and truthful proof precede guarantees

Original research status: supported.

Do not borrow another provider results or fabricate testimonials, certainty or effort.

* [Offer and cold traffic constraints](https://www.clientascension.io/blogs/if-i-restarted-my-10m-agency-today-this-is-the-only-offer-i-d-sell). Author: Daniel Fazio. Locator: What Does Not Work, point 4. Ground the pitch in actual outcomes and proof; diagnose funnel constraints separately.
* [Capability before promises](https://x.com/danielfazio/status/1938286163040899419). Author: Daniel Fazio. Locator: Post text. Credible capability and actual results matter more than unsupported promises.
* [Truth in claims](https://x.com/danielfazio/status/1938963960616702111). Author: Daniel Fazio. Locator: Post text. Keep testimonials, expected outcomes and effort requirements accurate.

### Consider bounded deal terms

Original research status: candidate.

Evaluate buyer risk and delivery economics before offering pilots or guarantees.

* [Deal structure as differentiation](https://x.com/danielfazio/status/2101281648843129311). Author: Daniel Fazio. Locator: Post text. A carefully bounded pilot or contract structure can reduce decision risk when economics permit.
