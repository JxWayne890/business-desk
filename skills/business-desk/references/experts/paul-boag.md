## Methods

### Define customer needs before choosing the solution

Original research status: supported.

Identify the audience, tasks, questions, and business objective. Let the client's actual service or buying journey shape the proposed site.

* [Strategic User Driven Project Assessment](https://boagworld.com/emails/introducing-supa/). Author: Paul Boag. Locator: Selling SUPA to Your Organization or Clients. Assess the audience, genuine need, feasibility, and scope before committing to the solution. Select research activities according to the project. Use findings to prioritize recommendations. The essay describes a process, not a guarantee that a website will convert or a requirement to run every listed research activity.
* [Customer Journey Mapping](https://boagworld.com/usability/customer-journey-mapping/). Author: Paul Boag. Locator: Identify the key stages of user interaction. Map the principal audience's tasks, questions, touchpoints, and friction over time. Set the scope to the question being investigated and vary the stages with the actual service or product. A map is a useful archetype, not a perfect account of every customer. Illustrative revenue claims were not used as performance promises.

### Scope the journey map to the decision being made

### Judge the design system across real page contexts

Original research status: candidate.

Try the system with realistic content and page variations before refining isolated components. Verify the relevant journeys separately.

* [Public teaching post 2090393903845224696](https://x.com/boagworld/status/2090393903845224696). Author: Paul Boag. Locator: Author text in root post 2090393903845224696. Evaluate the visual system across real page types and content variations before spending too long on isolated details. The author describes his own experience, not a controlled performance study.
