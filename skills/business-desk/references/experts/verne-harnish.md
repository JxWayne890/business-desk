## Methods

### Use brief huddles to expose priorities and blockers

Original research status: supported.

Use brief management check ins to identify priorities, measures, and obstacles, then assign follow through outside the meeting. Adapt timing to crew schedules and operating needs.

* [Sales Fundamentals: Daily Meetings](https://scalingup.com/blog/sales-fundamentals-daily-meetings). Author: Verne Harnish. Locator: Focus on Stuck Points section. Use a short regular huddle to expose blockers and coordinate a response. This is a management routine, not proof of a guaranteed sales increase.
* [How an AI startup used the Scaling Up platform to increase profits](https://scalingup.com/blog/how-an-ai-startup-used-the-scaling-up-platform-to-increase-profits). Author: Verne Harnish. Locator: Getting clear on strategy section. Harnish reports a team using brief daily meetings to maintain attention on priorities. The case illustrates his operating approach; it is not a controlled evaluation.
* [Connect priorities, metrics, and blockers in a huddle](https://x.com/agilescaleup/status/1882428573861982490). Author: Verne Harnish. Locator: Authored X post 1882428573861982490. Review today’s priority, a small set of useful measures, and obstacles requiring action. Adapt the cadence to the team.
