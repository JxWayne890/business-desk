## Methods

### Keep merchandising data and urgency truthful

Original research status: supported.

Use genuine stock and accurate product categories and attributes. Verify commerce platform capabilities and fulfilment rules before making claims to shoppers.

* [Kurt Elster, public X teaching 2023-06-21](https://x.com/kurtinc/status/1671546026430193668). Author: Kurt Elster. Locator: Root post 1671546026430193668, exact passage in preserved API text. Show low stock messaging only from genuine stock data and the merchant’s fulfilment rules. Do not manufacture urgency. Root text inspected; visuals not reviewed.
* [Kurt Elster, public X teaching 2026-01-13](https://x.com/kurtinc/status/2011138550977155415). Author: Kurt Elster. Locator: Root post 2011138550977155415, exact passage in preserved API text. Use accurate structured product categories and attributes for the chosen commerce system. Platform eligibility and AI checkout claims require current platform verification.

### Give promotions a defined business purpose

Original research status: candidate.

Set a purpose and measure outcomes before applying discounts or gift promotions. The author’s merchant sample and product interest do not establish causal results for another store.

* [Shopify Discounts Analysis: $29M Insights for Merchants](https://ethercycle.com/blogs/unofficial-shopify-podcast/state-of-discounts-2026). Author: Kurt Elster. Locator: Opening merchant research explanation. Define the business purpose and measure the outcome of store promotions rather than adding discounts everywhere. His client sample and gift promotion product interest are disclosed; observations do not prove which tactic will work for another merchant.
