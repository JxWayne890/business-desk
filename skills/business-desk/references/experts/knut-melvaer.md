## Methods

### Model the actual business content early

Original research status: supported.

Represent services, products, variants, availability, people and relationships around real customer tasks and editorial responsibility. Do not flatten every business into a page template.

* [Why content modeling early unlocks value | Sanity](https://www.sanity.io/blog/why-content-modeling-early-unlocks-value). Author: Ronald Aveling and Knut Melvær. Locator: Content supports intent. Model the business content early around what customers need to do. Structured product, service and scheduling information should support the journey and editor workflow rather than be trapped in a page design.
* [The 10 laws of developer experience for content management systems | Knut Melvær](https://www.knut.fyi/blog/2026-06-04/the-10-laws-of-developer-experience-for-content-management-systems). Author: Knut Melvær. Locator: III. Your content model should be as expressive as your domain. Choose a content model expressive enough for actual variants, prices, availability and relationships. This is the author’s stated view with a declared vendor bias. Do not install Sanity automatically or treat Markdown as universally unsuitable.

### Protect content relationships during changes

Original research status: candidate.

Use references and migration checks where the chosen system supports them. Test consuming routes before content schema changes and deletion.

* [Internal and external links with Portable Text | Sanity.io guide](https://www.sanity.io/guides/portable-text-internal-and-external-links). Author: Knut Melvær. Locator: Opening internal versus external link explanation. Use content references for internal relationships when the chosen CMS supports them. Validate broken references and preview behavior. The historical Sanity examples require current platform syntax before implementation.
* [Knut Melvær, public X teaching 2022-10-04](https://x.com/kmelve/status/1577431063710040064). Author: Knut Melvær. Locator: Root post 1577431063710040064, exact passage in preserved API text. Model changes may break consuming sites. This is an experience question, not a measured incidence or proven universal claim. Test content migrations and references.

### Coordinate image crops with text legibility

Original research status: candidate.

Preserve focal points and check the actual image and text contrast across rendered crops. Treat the vendor example as optional.

* [Five interesting ways to use Sanity.io for image art direction | CSS-Tricks](https://css-tricks.com/five-interesting-ways-to-use-sanity-io-for-image-art-direction/). Author: Knut Melvær. Locator: 1. Grab the palette from image metadata. Use intentional image and text relationships and preserve focal points when transforming images. Verify contrast on the rendered crop. The vendor image API is an example, not a compulsory client dependency.
