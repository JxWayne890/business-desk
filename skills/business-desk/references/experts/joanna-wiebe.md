## Methods

### Research the message before applying a formula

Original research status: supported.

Use actual customer language, problems, objections and the client’s verifiable difference to establish the message. A formula organizes that evidence.

* [Copywriting Formulas - the ultimate guide. 200+ examples.](https://copyhackers.com/2015/10/copywriting-formula/). Author: Joanna Wiebe. Locator: First and Fast: How Do Copywriting Formulas Make Copy So Much Better?. Use a relevant framework to organize researched messages before refining headings, value, proof and buttons. A formula cannot supply missing customer evidence. Historical examples are not contemporary interface requirements.
* [A super-speedy formula to find VoC fast | Write like a human | Convert like a badass](https://copyhackers.com/a-super-speedy-formula-to-find-voc-fast/). Author: Joanna Wiebe. Locator: Transcript, Joanna Wiebe opening explanation. Start from customer language and actual problems. This written transcript demonstrates one review research approach. Reviews require context and permission; do not invent client testimonials or claim the video was watched.

### Build a hierarchy around the visitor’s questions

Original research status: supported.

Write the hero, proof and next action around awareness and the principal task. Adapt the sequence when the business and landing page require it.

* [3 copywriting principles (for 2x revenue)](https://copyhackers.com/2016/06/copywriting-principles-sweatblock/). Author: Joanna Wiebe. Locator: Messaging hierarchy and The 10/90 Messaging Layout Rule. Sequence the page around the visitor’s initial awareness, questions, skepticism, and intended action. Her seven questions are a starting point, with explicit exceptions, rather than a mandatory section template.
* [Copywriting Formulas - the ultimate guide. 200+ examples.](https://copyhackers.com/2015/10/copywriting-formula/). Author: Joanna Wiebe. Locator: First and Fast: How Do Copywriting Formulas Make Copy So Much Better?. Use a relevant framework to organize researched messages before refining headings, value, proof and buttons. A formula cannot supply missing customer evidence. Historical examples are not contemporary interface requirements.

### Substantiate the business difference

Original research status: candidate.

Inspect competitors for generic claims, then write a difference the actual client can prove. Do not infer conversion success from appearance.

* [Differentiation and Your Home Page Visitors | Copy Hackers](https://copyhackers.com/2013/02/differentiation/). Author: Joanna Wiebe. Locator: Opening competitor comparison. Competitor copy can conceal poor performance. Examine it for undifferentiated claims, then find a difference the actual business can substantiate. The dated product screenshots do not establish current competitor behavior.
