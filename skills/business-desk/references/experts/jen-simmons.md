### Consider reading and focus order when arranging galleries

Original research status: candidate.

For visually packed collections, test how keyboard focus and reading order travel through the actual items. This candidate reflects joint WebKit teaching.

* [Introducing CSS Grid Lanes](https://webkit.org/blog/17660/introducing-css-grid-lanes/). Author: Jen Simmons, Elika Etemad, and Brandon Stewart. Locator: Flow Tolerance, final recommendation. The team describes flexible gallery arrangements and the consequences of visual packing for reading and keyboard order. This is explicitly joint teaching. Individual authorship of every passage is unknown. The article describes technology preview behavior, so browser availability and fallback must be checked before using the syntax in a client website.
