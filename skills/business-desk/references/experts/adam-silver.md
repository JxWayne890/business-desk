## Methods

### Remove unnecessary work before polishing forms

Original research status: supported.

Design the service around the desired outcome and remove avoidable upload or data entry requirements. Confirm what information is actually necessary.

* [The first law of form design – Adam Silver – designer, London, UK](https://adamsilver.io/blog/the-first-law-of-form-design/). Author: Adam Silver. Locator: The first law of form design. Design forms to help people complete the real task and return to their work. Entertainment and novelty do not replace completion, clear questions, and respectful effort.
* [The perfect file upload pattern – Adam Silver – designer, London, UK](https://adamsilver.io/blog/the-perfect-file-upload-pattern/). Author: Adam Silver. Locator: Driving license example and upload effort. Remove unnecessary work at the service level before polishing the widget. Reuse already available information only when access and the person’s choice permit it. Do not require an upload merely to make intake look sophisticated.

### Provide a discoverable path to recover from mistakes

Original research status: supported.

Implement validation and clear error recovery without hiding the reason an action cannot proceed. Test retained values and the corrected submission.

* [The problem with disabled buttons and what to do instead – Adam Silver – designer, London, UK](https://adamsilver.io/blog/the-problem-with-disabled-buttons-and-what-to-do-instead/). Author: Adam Silver. Locator: Problem #1: They don’t give feedback. A button disabled by unreported validation leaves users without a path to diagnose the problem. Provide discoverable validation and recovery. This free article does not disclose every pattern in the paid course.
* [Adam Silver, public X teaching 2025-11-18](https://x.com/adamsilverhq/status/1990755634946715915). Author: Adam Silver. Locator: Root post 1990755634946715915, exact passage in preserved API text. Plan discoverable validation, error presentation and recovery. This root text describes decisions to make, not a universal answer for every form.

### Observe question time and pause time

Original research status: candidate.

Evaluate uncertainty, consequences and answering effort in the actual journey. Fewer fields alone do not prove faster or easier completion.

* [The second law of form design – Adam Silver – designer, London, UK](https://adamsilver.io/blog/the-second-law-of-form-design/). Author: Adam Silver. Locator: The second law of form design. Examine both the effort of answering and the pauses caused by uncertainty, review, and submission consequences. Optional fields may still impose effort. Use observation of the actual journey rather than counting fields alone.
