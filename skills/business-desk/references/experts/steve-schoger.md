## Methods

### Create hierarchy through deliberate type and contrast

Original research status: supported.

Make the important content more prominent with typography, spacing, and contrast. Inspect the result at the actual viewing size.

* [Public teaching post 877558258755612672](https://x.com/steveschoger/status/877558258755612672). Author: Steve Schoger. Locator: Author text in root post 877558258755612672. Typography, spacing, and contrast can establish hierarchy in an otherwise plain interface. The accompanying text is the evidence; it does not establish a numerical sizing rule.
* [Public teaching post 870679289624092673](https://x.com/steveschoger/status/870679289624092673). Author: Steve Schoger. Locator: Author text in root post 870679289624092673. Vary prominence with color and contrast as well as size and weight. The attached vehicle themed graphic was visually inspected and uses darker headings with lighter body text.

### Separate the option name from supporting detail

Original research status: candidate.

Give product, plan, or service choices a clear name and useful description. This candidate comes from a specific plan selection example.

* [Public teaching post 1260223160588386304](https://x.com/steveschoger/status/1260223160588386304). Author: Steve Schoger. Locator: Author text in root post 1260223160588386304. An inspected plan selection graphic separates concise option names from supporting descriptions. The graphic is branded Refactoring UI and is treated as book related evidence. This is a presentation method, not proof of conversion.

### Consider tonal separation before adding another border

Original research status: candidate.

Use a subtle surface contrast when it clarifies a group or heading. Preserve visible boundaries where they carry necessary meaning.

* [Public teaching post 871757453033132034](https://x.com/steveschoger/status/871757453033132034). Author: Steve Schoger. Locator: Author text in root post 871757453033132034. Consider a subtle tonal difference to distinguish panel headings when another border would add clutter. This single case does not establish a universal prohibition on borders.

### Check the spacing of short uppercase labels

Original research status: candidate.

Consider extra letter spacing when uppercase text feels cramped, then inspect the chosen typeface and size.

* [Public teaching post 869932734466195456](https://x.com/steveschoger/status/869932734466195456). Author: Steve Schoger. Locator: Author text in root post 869932734466195456. Short uppercase text can need additional tracking. Check the actual font and reading context rather than applying one tracking value everywhere.
