## Methods

### Verify analytics events with positive and negative cases

Original research status: supported.

Test intended events and parameters at the receiving system and cases that must not fire. Distinguish an action click from a completed business outcome.

* [How to Track Clicks with Google Analytics 4 (2026)](https://www.analyticsmania.com/post/track-clicks-with-google-analytics-4-and-gtm/). Author: Julius Fedorovicius. Locator: Test your click tracking in Google Analytics 4. Validate events and parameters at their receiving analytics destination. A clicked navigation link is behavioral data, not automatically a qualified lead or completed booking.
* [Track Outbound Links with Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics](https://www.analyticsmania.com/post/track-outbound-links-with-google-tag-manager/). Author: Julius Fedorovicius. Locator: Step 4. Test. Test both events that should fire and events that must not. Check internal, external, email and telephone cases, then inspect the data arriving in GA4. Match definitions to the business goal.

### Verify consent initialization before relevant tags

Original research status: candidate.

Configure and test consent order and state transitions for the approved measurement setup. Confirm legal policy separately when it is required.

* [[Solved] A tag read consent state before a default was set. What to do about it? - Analytics Mania](https://www.analyticsmania.com/post/a-tag-read-consent-state-before-a-default-was-set/). Author: Julius Fedorovicius. Locator: What does this error mean?. Set and verify consent state in the intended order before relevant tags execute. Analytics consent configuration is not itself a determination of the legal obligations for a client’s jurisdiction.

### Measure the cost of third party tags

Original research status: candidate.

Inspect vendor code and firing rules using actual performance observations. Asynchronous execution is not a guarantee of no performance cost.

* [Google Tag Manager vs Page Speed: Impact & How to Improve](https://www.analyticsmania.com/post/google-tag-manager-impact-on-page-speed-and-how-to-improve/). Author: Julius Fedorovicius. Locator: Results and Insights. Measure third party tag costs and audit the vendors and firing rules inside the container. Do not assume a tag manager automatically speeds up every website or that delaying all tags preserves measurement.
