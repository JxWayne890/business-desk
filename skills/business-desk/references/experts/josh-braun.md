## Methods

### Reduce pressure in the first conversation

Original research status: supported.

Use an easy relevant response and accept that the offer may not fit.

* [15 Cold Email Copywriting Principles](https://joshbraun.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/15coldemail-copywriting.pdf). Author: Josh Braun. Locator: PDF page 12, Slow Down. Make the first response easy; use recognizable buyer language and specific outcomes.
* [Stop persuading](https://joshbraun.com/). Author: Josh Braun. Locator: My Mission. Treat the buyer as someone to understand rather than someone to force into agreement.

### Ask for interest before a large time commitment
