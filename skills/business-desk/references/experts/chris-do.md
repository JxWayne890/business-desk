## Methods

### Establish hierarchy and a deliberate reading order

Original research status: supported.

Use scale, contrast, spacing, and alignment to make the main message and its supporting details easy to read.

* [How to Improve Your Layout and Typography Critique](https://thefutur.com/content/how-to-improve-your-layout-and-typography). Author: Chris Do. Locator: Opening and five fundamentals. Typography communicates through contrast, scale, repetition, balance, and hierarchy. Choose type for the message rather than appearance alone.
* [Learning How to Use Typography in Seven Weeks](https://thefutur.com/content/learning-how-to-use-typography). Author: Chris Do. Locator: Critique checklist and Week 3 Purpose and function. Set a focal point and reading order; use spacing and alignment intentionally. Zooming out is part of the written critique process. Week 2 onward includes Milka Broukhim and must preserve her attribution.

### Keep each carousel slide focused

Original research status: candidate.

Use one useful idea per slide and a coherent visual sequence. Adapt to the platform’s actual document or multi image format.

* [Improve Your Instagram Carousel Design](https://thefutur.com/watch/instagram-carousel-design-content-marketing-advice). Author: Chris Do. Locator: Mistake 2 Information Overload and call to action critique. The publisher’s written critique describes a clear opening, flow, readable contrast, coherent styling, light but useful content, and one closing action. Treat the historical Instagram recommendations as design methods to adapt and test, not current algorithm rules.
