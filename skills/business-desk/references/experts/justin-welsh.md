## Methods

### Use simple language and obvious reader context

Original research status: supported.

Write one understandable idea for the intended reader. Explain unfamiliar terminology and make the value visible early.

* [Revealed: The Social Media Game](https://justinwelsh.me/essays/the-social-media-game-revealed). Author: Justin Welsh. Locator: Level I and Level II. Keep beginner explanations simple. Review the audience’s response and adapt strong ideas into new formats. His audience growth account and collaboration suggestions are personal experience, not evidence of guaranteed reach.
* [The Number One Reason Content Fails](https://justinwelsh.me/essays/the-1-reason-content-fails). Author: Justin Welsh. Locator: Today’s action step, opening line questions. An opening should make the intended reader, topic, and relevance clear. The article compares two posts, but that anecdote cannot isolate the cause of their different outcomes.

### Use response and business results to guide iteration

Original research status: supported.

Study patterns across comparable posts and track qualified conversations, inquiries, and sales. Treat a single popular post as a clue, not proof.

* [Revealed: The Social Media Game](https://justinwelsh.me/essays/the-social-media-game-revealed). Author: Justin Welsh. Locator: Level I and Level II. Keep beginner explanations simple. Review the audience’s response and adapt strong ideas into new formats. His audience growth account and collaboration suggestions are personal experience, not evidence of guaranteed reach.
* [Measure prospects](https://x.com/thejustinwelsh/status/1751229304367644716). Author: Justin Welsh. Locator: Post 1751229304367644716, author text. He recommends measuring the sources of qualified prospects, including tracked links, before concentrating effort. Likes alone are insufficient for the owner’s lead generation goal.
* [Learn from response](https://x.com/thejustinwelsh/status/1717636805338492951). Author: Justin Welsh. Locator: Post 1717636805338492951, author text. He recommends helping people, observing response, and adjusting without waiting for a perfect long range plan. Audience feedback is not a substitute for checking factual accuracy.

### Combine real observations with a bank of structures

Original research status: candidate.

Create a small idea bank from actual work and pair each idea with an appropriate explanatory form.

* [Ideas and structures](https://x.com/thejustinwelsh/status/1349029508410728450). Author: Justin Welsh. Locator: Post 1349029508410728450, author text. He combines a list of opinions with a list of structures, drafts, edits, and publishes. Use original business observations rather than copying his views.

### Draft the substance before polishing the opening

Original research status: candidate.

Make the useful idea concrete first, then create an opening that represents it and a closing that helps the reader. Three excerpts from one thread count as one work.

* [Linkedin substance first](https://x.com/thejustinwelsh/status/1995478607641112977). Author: Justin Welsh. Locator: Post 1995478607641112977, author text. The author says he writes the teaching substance before polishing the opening. This is one workflow from a single LinkedIn thread, not a universal sequence.
* [Linkedin opening jobs](https://x.com/thejustinwelsh/status/1995478617418051711). Author: Justin Welsh. Locator: Post 1995478617418051711, author text. He gives the opening two jobs, interrupting scrolling and encouraging the next line to be read. Use a truthful opening that fits the body rather than exaggerated bait.
* [Linkedin takeaway](https://x.com/thejustinwelsh/status/1995478627190755806). Author: Justin Welsh. Locator: Post 1995478627190755806, author text. He closes with a useful takeaway and a relevant conversation question. Every post does not need a question if a clear service next step better fits its goal.
