## Methods

### Connect spending to measurable business returns

Original research status: supported.

Before spending on advertising or AI work, state the expected benefit and compare actual returns with cost. Evidence comes from separate 2022 advertising advice and a 2026 AI memo.

* [Tie AI spending to business returns](https://x.com/AlexHormozi/status/2105446365119525013). Author: Alex Hormozi. Locator: X post 2105446365119525013, authored post text, published 2026-09-30. State the expected business benefit of an AI workflow and measure whether the spend produces that benefit.
* [Compare advertising cost with customer economics](https://x.com/AlexHormozi/status/1607455631908442113). Author: Alex Hormozi. Locator: X post 1607455631908442113, authored post text, published 2022-12-26. Compare acquisition cost with contribution after delivery costs, including the observed retention period.
