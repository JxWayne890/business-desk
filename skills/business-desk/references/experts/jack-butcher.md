## Methods

### Build recognition through visual consistency

Original research status: supported.

Keep a limited palette, type system, spacing, and layout logic for recurring graphic series. Develop the owner’s own identity instead of copying Visualize Value.

* [How to Create Visual Leverage](https://visualizevalue.com/blog/how-to-create-visual-leverage). Author: Jack Butcher. Locator: Be consistent and Make the intangible tangible. Use recognizable symbols, show processes, and structure information with hierarchy. Build a recognizable visual system. The 2020 Visual leverage posts and this later article are treated as one underlying work.
* [Personal Brands vs Company Brands](https://visualizevalue.com/blog/personal-brands-vs-company-brands). Author: Jack Butcher. Locator: Personal and project brand discussion. A narrow and consistent visual feed can establish a recognizable project. Personal accounts and project brands serve different relationships. The explanations are the author’s hypotheses from shared practice with Celia Butcher, not a controlled comparison.

### Choose simplicity deliberately

Original research status: candidate.

Reduce elements until the intended relation is clear. This does not imply that every subject needs minimal styling.

* [Simplicity](https://x.com/jackbutcher/status/1388524378011414528). Author: Jack Butcher. Locator: Author text in post 1388524378011414528. Simplicity requires intentional design. This is a short statement, not a complete process.

### Let the idea guide the image

Original research status: candidate.

Decide what to communicate before choosing effects or creating art.

* [Idea first](https://x.com/jackbutcher/status/1663187112940716036). Author: Jack Butcher. Locator: Author text in post 1663187112940716036. The author prioritizes the idea over the image. Apply as a design priority, not a claim that images are unimportant.
