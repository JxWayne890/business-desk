## Methods

### Concrete language and one main idea

Original research status: supported.

Make the offer understandable in a quick reading; punctuation is not the objective.

* [The One Idea Rule](https://lavender.ai/blog/stop-using-commas-in-emails). Author: Will Allred. Locator: The one idea rule. Keep one central message and remove sentences that obscure it.
* [Concrete buyer language](https://x.com/WillAllred117/status/1864494963633094663). Author: Will Allred. Locator: Post text. Translate abstract sales language into a specific job the reader recognizes.

### Explain the connection from evidence to offer

Original research status: candidate.

Use observations as context; label uncertain business implications as hypotheses.

* [Research must explain relevance](https://x.com/WillAllred117/status/1740400037182546337). Author: Will Allred. Locator: Post text. Connect a verified observation to the reason for contact and leave room for the hypothesis to be wrong.

### Confidence about capability, humility about the buyer

Original research status: candidate.

Avoid telling a stranger their business is broken.

* [Tentative about the buyer](https://x.com/WillAllred117/status/1867601869775700065). Author: Will Allred. Locator: Post text. Be clear about the actual service while making uncertainty about the buyer explicit.

### Restrain repeated outreach

Original research status: supported.

Use finite sequences, add useful information and respect replies or stop requests.

* [Respectful sequences](https://x.com/WillAllred117/status/1724881499470602429). Author: Will Allred. Locator: Post text. Avoid follow ups designed to irritate someone into replying.
* [Restrain cadence and track replies](https://x.com/WillAllred117/status/1873203919561859163). Author: Will Allred. Locator: Post text. Prioritize replies and avoid repeatedly contacting the same person or account with the same message.

### Test one conceptual variable

Original research status: candidate.

Define the hypothesis and control, then measure without overreading sparse results.

* [Running better experiments](https://lavender.ai/blog/quit-guessing-your-guide-to-running-better-experiments). Author: Will Allred. Locator: More Rules for Running a Great Experiment. Use a hypothesis, control and one conceptual change; treat small samples as directional.
