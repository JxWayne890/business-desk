### Use spacing to express content relationships

Original research status: candidate.

Keep related information close and separate unrelated groups. Choose spacing values that remain consistent across this client's interface.

* [Fourteen logical UI design tips](https://www.adhamdannaway.com/blog/ui-design/ui-design-tips-14). Author: Adham Dannaway. Locator: Space elements based on how closely related they are. Use spacing to express relationships, make the primary action visible, keep essential actions discoverable, and supplement color with other cues for state. The article demonstrates these changes in a community profile example. Its numerical accessibility recommendations are historical source statements and require verification against current standards before production use.
