## Methods

### Direct the subject and camera

Original research status: supported.

Specify the intended subject behavior and framing, then judge the resulting visual properties.

* [Photography foundations before prompt tricks](https://x.com/dcbruck/status/1686521919326081024). Author: Drew Brucker. Locator: Author text in post 1686521919326081024. Know which visual properties to judge even when using an image upload, description, or blend. This is a short reply with limited surrounding context.
* [Creative levers, camera and subject](https://x.com/dcbruck/status/1986480028352163959). Author: Drew Brucker. Locator: Author text in post 1986480028352163959. Choose framing and subject behavior deliberately. Objects, gestures, empty space, and color can carry an idea.

### Use material detail and appropriate imperfection

Original research status: supported.

Describe tactile surfaces. For photorealism, allow plausible irregularities while preserving useful clarity.

* [Creative levers, dimension and material](https://x.com/dcbruck/status/1985504393127399775). Author: Drew Brucker. Locator: Author text in post 1985504393127399775. The author treats prompting as directing. Texture and film character contribute deliberate qualities to an image.
* [Realism through appropriate imperfection](https://x.com/dcbruck/status/1968019789986795669). Author: Drew Brucker. Locator: Author text in post 1968019789986795669. For realistic photographs, controlled natural irregularities can reduce a synthetic polished appearance. This does not require flaws in every illustration or brand graphic.

### Make the frame serve the idea

Original research status: supported.

Set subject scale, placement, and empty space before generating. Frame choice changes emphasis.

* [Creative levers, camera and subject](https://x.com/dcbruck/status/1986480028352163959). Author: Drew Brucker. Locator: Author text in post 1986480028352163959. Choose framing and subject behavior deliberately. Objects, gestures, empty space, and color can carry an idea.
* [Frame and aspect ratio with purpose](https://x.com/dcbruck/status/1948398356360839514). Author: Drew Brucker. Locator: Author text in post 1948398356360839514. The author associates different frames with breadth, vertical depth, and space for a layout. These are creative heuristics, not mandatory Facebook or LinkedIn sizes.

### Choose photographic character for a reason

Original research status: supported.

Use compatible grain, contrast, color response, and period cues when they serve the scene. Film styling is optional.

* [Creative levers, dimension and material](https://x.com/dcbruck/status/1985504393127399775). Author: Drew Brucker. Locator: Author text in post 1985504393127399775. The author treats prompting as directing. Texture and film character contribute deliberate qualities to an image.
* [Film character as a style ingredient](https://x.com/dcbruck/status/1867247946812588329). Author: Drew Brucker. Locator: Author text in post 1867247946812588329. Film character can inform mood and period. For other generators, describe color response, grain, and contrast in natural language instead of promising the same named stock result.

### Review and finish generated work

Original research status: supported.

Inspect several visible quality criteria and refine the result. A generated image is a starting point for review.

* [Image quality needs several criteria](https://x.com/dcbruck/status/1821171411001422263). Author: Drew Brucker. Locator: Author text in post 1821171411001422263. Evaluate visible detail, material, contrast, and clarity separately. The historical version comparison is not a ranking of current models.
* [Generation is a foundation for finishing](https://x.com/dcbruck/status/2077833295522382290). Author: Drew Brucker. Locator: Author text in post 2077833295522382290. The author recommends treating generated imagery as material for further finishing. This supports deliberate review; it does not establish a particular editing recipe.

### Select a relevant photography type

Original research status: candidate.

Choose an editorial, street, portrait, or still life approach for its relevant properties. Avoid incompatible styles.

* [Photography type changes the visual narrative](https://x.com/dcbruck/status/1866506316836921813). Author: Drew Brucker. Locator: Author text in post 1866506316836921813. Choose a photographic approach that fits the scene and feeling. This edition reviewed the root teaching text, not all the pocket guide panels.

### Build reusable prompts from tested work

Original research status: candidate.

Keep a successful structure and adapt the subject and message. Validate a pilot before making it a standard.

* [Pinterest creative infrastructure case study](https://drewcbrucker.com/work/pinterest). Author: Drew Brucker. Locator: The approach, paragraphs 1 and 3. The authored case study describes testing several visual forms against brand standards, then building reusable prompts and documentation. This demonstrates described commercial practice, not access to the private curriculum.
