## Methods

### Choose deliberate visuals that fit the brand

Original research status: supported.

Use a coherent motif or brand appropriate visual technique. Establish legibility and hierarchy before adding expressive details.

* [Visual motifs for a more cohesive brand](https://www.learnui.design/blog/spice-up-designs-create-cohesive-brand.html). Author: Erik D. Kennedy. Locator: Introducing Motifs. Repeat a meaningful visual idea through typography, dividers, imagery, and other brand details. The article analyzes Foundation Medicine and The Intercept, and starts from an already legible, well spaced design. This is commentary on those examples, not authorship of their websites. The visible update date is June 15, 2024.
* [Do What Is Hard To Do In Figma, A Design Manifesto](https://www.learnui.design/blog/hard-in-figma.html). Author: Erik D. Kennedy. Locator: Opening examples of moving beyond rectangular defaults. Tool defaults influence visual sameness. Consider brand appropriate materials, custom photography, illustration, or other deliberate techniques to give the site a distinctive voice. This is an invitation to explore, not a requirement to add animation, WebGL, or heavy assets. Tool and library recommendations need current verification before implementation.

### Place controls near the thing they change

Original research status: candidate.

Keep product options, inventory filters, and actions understandable in context. Check placement with the intended customer task.

* [Four Rules for Intuitive UX](https://www.learnui.design/blog/4-rules-intuitive-ux.html). Author: Erik D. Kennedy. Locator: Obey the Law of Locality. Place controls near the content they affect. Give the intended task visual priority. Explain unfamiliar products through concrete examples, and choose input controls according to the decision being made. The examples are teaching cases, not current audits of the named products. Dropdown advice is contextual and should not become a blanket prohibition.

### Adjust tracking according to the typeface and text role

Original research status: supported.

Consider tighter tracking for an appropriate large title and looser tracking for short uppercase labels. Do not make one value or treatment mandatory for all brands.

* [Public teaching post 2013317499937530147](https://x.com/erikdkennedy/status/2013317499937530147). Author: Erik D. Kennedy. Locator: Author text in root post 2013317499937530147. The author comments on an Evernote header and its Figtree treatment. This is one font and context, with no evidence that the same adjustment suits all typefaces.
* [Public teaching post 1806357012030337321](https://x.com/erikdkennedy/status/1806357012030337321). Author: Erik D. Kennedy. Locator: Author text in root post 1806357012030337321. The author describes tracking choices for uppercase labels and large titles. Apply these choices only after looking at the actual font, brand, size, and legibility.
