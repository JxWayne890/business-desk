### Check fetched and rendered content before diagnosing quality

Original research status: candidate.

Verify the actual page response, assets and rendered content. Treat indexing diagnoses as case dependent and defer implementation requirements to current official search documentation.

* [Why your pages are stuck in crawled-currently not indexed. And what to do about it. - Marie Haynes](https://www.mariehaynes.com/crawled-currently-not-indexed/). Author: Marie Haynes. Locator: 1.
