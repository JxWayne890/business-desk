## Methods

### Give the intended reader a reason to care

Original research status: supported.

Lead with a recognisable concern and explain why the idea matters. For a local business, address the right buyer rather than aiming for the broadest audience.

* [How To Write Authentic Content](https://thedankoe.com/letters/dont-get-replaced-by-ai-how-to-write-authentic-content/). Author: Dan Koe. Locator: Actionable Principles; Importance Of A Topic; Study The Best Content Structures. Organize a useful idea around an opening, explanation, and payoff. Ask why the reader should care and study effective communication forms. Its platform transfer claims are historical opinions, not current algorithm rules.
* [Reader reason](https://x.com/thedankoe/status/1921569918132289875). Author: Dan Koe. Locator: Post 1921569918132289875, author text. The author emphasizes the reader’s reason to care. His numerical claim is rhetorical and is not adopted as a measured causal effect.

### Draft ideas and improve through practice

Original research status: supported.

Avoid endless preparation. Perform a concise quality review before a business post is delivered.

* [Practice ideas](https://x.com/thedankoe/status/2103473370662617123). Author: Dan Koe. Locator: Post 2103473370662617123, author text. His recent post urges drafting an idea and improving it through practice. Tone and profanity are his own and should not be imported into the user’s business voice.
* [Publish and improve](https://x.com/thedankoe/status/1710330898229719296). Author: Dan Koe. Locator: Post 1710330898229719296, author text. He favors completing and publishing work over perfection. This differs from Dickie’s deliberate editing workflow. For business posts, preserve momentum while checking facts, claims, and spelling.

### Use AI as a critical reader

Original research status: candidate.

Check reader comprehension and objections. the user can still request AI drafting; preserve his facts and judgment.

* [Ai as reader](https://x.com/thedankoe/status/1912540916369543332). Author: Dan Koe. Locator: Post 1912540916369543332, author text. He describes using AI to challenge an argument and check comprehension while retaining authorship and enjoyment of writing. This is a personal workflow, not a prohibition on the user requesting AI drafts.
