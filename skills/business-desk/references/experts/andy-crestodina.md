## Methods

### Make the page specific to its business and action

Original research status: supported.

Answer real visitor questions and inspect headings, imagery, navigation and action destinations for meaningful business details.

* [Lead Generation 101: How to Increase Conversion Rates on Your B2B Website](https://www.orbitmedia.com/blog/increase-conversion-rate/). Author: Andy Crestodina. Locator: Where to put your supportive evidence. Answer the visitor’s questions and objections, then place relevant proof alongside each claim. Interior landing pages need their own evidence. An example testimonial or conversion increase is not evidence about the user’s client.
* [The One Prompt That Improves Any Page …by Checking for 5 Types of Specificity | Orbit Media Studios](https://www.orbitmedia.com/blog/specificity-webpages/). Author: Andy Crestodina. Locator: Introduction and The Specificity Audit Prompt. Audit headings, hero image, subheads, navigation and actions for specificity to the business. Follow the action to check the promise against its destination. An AI audit is a proposed assessment, validated by actual people and browser behavior.

### Connect real proof to the claim it supports

Original research status: supported.

Use approved client experience, results and evidence alongside relevant claims. Do not turn example metrics or invented stories into client proof.

* [Lead Generation 101: How to Increase Conversion Rates on Your B2B Website](https://www.orbitmedia.com/blog/increase-conversion-rate/). Author: Andy Crestodina. Locator: Where to put your supportive evidence. Answer the visitor’s questions and objections, then place relevant proof alongside each claim. Interior landing pages need their own evidence. An example testimonial or conversion increase is not evidence about the user’s client.
* [2 Types of Content AI Can’t Create (And Why They Win) | Orbit Media Studios](https://www.orbitmedia.com/blog/content-ai-cant-make/). Author: Andy Crestodina. Locator: Why can’t AI do this?. Use the client’s real experience and original research as inputs. AI can assist with processing these inputs but cannot establish that a real client experienced an event or achieved an unprovided result. The author’s broader assertions about all AI behavior are opinions, not technical facts.

### Measure business outcomes rather than superficial activity

Original research status: candidate.

Define a qualified enquiry, booking or sale and verify its downstream evidence. Treat channel comparisons as contextual observations.

* [AI Traffic Conversion Rates: New Research from 97 B2B Websites and 29M visits | Orbit Media Studios](https://www.orbitmedia.com/blog/conversion-rates-ai-search/). Author: Andy Crestodina. Locator: Opening research rationale and conversion definitions. Define meaningful business conversions before comparing channels. The author excluded mere clicks and page views from lead conversions in a 97 site B2B sample. Results are dated observational findings, not a guarantee or causal proof for another business.
