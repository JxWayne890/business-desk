# Consult a reference

Select the job using [business routing](business-routing.md). Check the [bundled catalog](experts/index.json) and any existing personal library. A bundled brief is a compiled summary with gaps; a personal library has its own profile, raw evidence, source index, and audit status.

For bundled knowledge, read the selected brief, then its relevant source summaries. Open the original links when making precise attributions, quoting, researching current facts, or going beyond the summary. Say when a source is inaccessible. For personal libraries, read `profile.json`, `wiki/hot.md`, and `wiki/index.md`, then at most five relevant pages initially. Expand only when the question needs more evidence.

Do not quietly replace an absent expert or say that a review passed just because a role was assigned. Continue authorized work using verified business facts and clearly identified general analysis when expert evidence is insufficient. A routine writing request does not require building a complete new research library.

Give the result first. Cite original work nearest an attributed claim. Keep citations outside customer copy when the user needs text to paste. Explain which part is your application of the source. Never manufacture a dialogue or imply that a real person reviewed the work.

For comparisons, preserve separate source attribution and meaningful disagreements. For teaching, explain a small relevant example at the user's level. For reviews, identify concrete issues, evidence, and proposed changes. For implementation, use current project instructions and meaningful verification after the last edit.

The package does not contain the creator's private X archive. Search the user's own saved evidence before paying to collect more. Do not save ordinary business conversations as expert beliefs; only newly inspected primary evidence can establish a new attributed method.
