## Selected source map

### W1. The One Idea Rule

[The One Idea Rule](https://lavender.ai/blog/stop-using-commas-in-emails). Will Allred. Published: 2023-05-10. Locator: The one idea rule.

Keep one central message and remove sentences that obscure it.

Limits: The literal comma ban and claimed numerical lift are not adopted as universal rules.

### W2. Running better experiments

[Running better experiments](https://lavender.ai/blog/quit-guessing-your-guide-to-running-better-experiments). Will Allred. Published: 2025-12-09. Locator: More Rules for Running a Great Experiment.

Use a hypothesis, control and one conceptual change; treat small samples as directional.

Limits: The example results are hypothetical. Lavender sample thresholds are not universal requirements.

### W3. Research must explain relevance

[Research must explain relevance](https://x.com/WillAllred117/status/1740400037182546337). Will Allred. Published: 2023-12-28. Locator: Post text.

Connect a verified observation to the reason for contact and leave room for the hypothesis to be wrong.

Limits: Do not treat AI speculation about a person as research.

### W4. Concrete buyer language

[Concrete buyer language](https://x.com/WillAllred117/status/1864494963633094663). Will Allred. Published: 2024-12-05. Locator: Post text.

Translate abstract sales language into a specific job the reader recognizes.

Limits: Do not repeat the claimed reading time or reply effect as established causal facts.

### W5. Tentative about the buyer

[Tentative about the buyer](https://x.com/WillAllred117/status/1867601869775700065). Will Allred. Published: 2024-12-13. Locator: Post text.

Be clear about the actual service while making uncertainty about the buyer explicit.

Limits: The aside about intensifying pain after a reply is excluded.

### W6. Respectful sequences

[Respectful sequences](https://x.com/WillAllred117/status/1724881499470602429). Will Allred. Published: 2023-11-15. Locator: Post text.

Avoid follow ups designed to irritate someone into replying.

Limits: Promotional statistics and old infrastructure prescriptions are excluded; channel expansion requires its own scope.

### W7. Restrain cadence and track replies

[Restrain cadence and track replies](https://x.com/WillAllred117/status/1873203919561859163). Will Allred. Published: 2024-12-29. Locator: Post text.

Prioritize replies and avoid repeatedly contacting the same person or account with the same message.

Limits: The stated daily send limit and sequence length are author heuristics, not provider limits or universal defaults.

### J1. 15 Cold Email Copywriting Principles

[15 Cold Email Copywriting Principles](https://joshbraun.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/15coldemail-copywriting.pdf). Josh Braun. Published: 2021-08. Locator: PDF page 12, Slow Down.

Make the first response easy; use recognizable buyer language and specific outcomes.

Limits: One PDF is one work. Numerical anecdotes and templates are not guarantees.

### J2. Stop persuading

[Stop persuading](https://joshbraun.com/). Josh Braun. Published: unknown. Locator: My Mission.

Treat the buyer as someone to understand rather than someone to force into agreement.

Limits: A public positioning essay supports tone, not a quantified campaign effect.

### B1. The cold email formula

[The cold email formula](https://www.30mpc.com/newsletter/the-data-backed-cold-email-formula-the-exact-words-length). Jason Bay. Published: 2025-07-10. Locator: Language and CTA sections.

Explain the buyer priority before the solution and make any meeting worth the buyer effort.

Limits: Reported Gong correlations are not a causal guarantee. Warm activity differs from true cold outreach.

### B2. Interview customers for messaging

[Interview customers for messaging](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/jasondbay_sales-prospecting-outbound-activity-6889914014915203072-rg8_). Jason Bay. Published: 2022-01. Locator: Author post text.

Use real customer conversations to learn priorities, previous approaches and stakes.

Limits: Only the author post was reviewed, not the attached video. Do not copy another client private details.

### H1. Core Session 1, Cold Emails

[Core Session 1, Cold Emails](https://8305887.fs1.hubspotusercontent-na1.net/hubfs/8305887/pdfs/New%202023/Core%202023/Flip%20the%20Script%20-%20Core%20Session%201_%20COLD%20EMAILS.pdf). Becc Holland. Published: 2023. Locator: PDF pages 9 to 15.

Limits: Deck text only, no video review. Corporate training material is attributed to Flip the Script teaching, not presumed sole authorship.

### H2. Five changes to cold emails

[Five changes to cold emails](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/beccholland-flipthescript_5-changes-that-i-made-to-my-cold-emails-activity-7460313099628359680-1a38). Becc Holland. Published: unknown. Locator: Author post, points 4 and 5.

Use a real reason to contact the person and ask concrete questions rather than loaded labels.

Limits: Exclude the open rate diagnostic threshold, fixed daily cap and performance claims. They do not establish inbox placement.

### H3. Relevance to buyer metrics

[Relevance to buyer metrics](https://www.linkedin.com/posts/beccholland-flipthescript_good-copy-comes-down-to-details-and-cold-activity-7021533931799789569-Ao34). Becc Holland. Published: 2023-01. Locator: Author post, question 2.

### A5. Concrete proof in templates

[Concrete proof in templates](https://www.alexberman.com/cold-email-templates). Alex Berman. Published: 2026-01-25. Locator: Case study discussion.

Limits: Same underlying work as A1. Do not copy its example results into another business pitch.

### D1. Offer and cold traffic constraints

Limits: His prescription that cold traffic only buys leads, calls or sales is rejected as a universal rule for agencies, software or other industries.

[Outcomes rather than a bundle](https://www.clientascension.io/blogs/if-i-restarted-my-10m-agency-today-this-is-the-only-offer-i-d-sell). Daniel Fazio. Published: 2026-03-30. Locator: The One Rule section.

## Evidence boundaries

A1 and A5 are excerpts from one work. D1 and D6 are excerpts from one work. Multiple excerpts do not provide independent corroboration. H1 is a Flip the Script deck with corporate attribution; it is not treated as proof of sole authorship. Videos, linked images and unreviewed thread replies do not establish methods here.

Marketing results are self reported unless independently validated. Exact publication dates remain unknown where the source does not establish them. A corpus may include replies, promotions and irrelevant material; only reviewed excerpts support this guide.

Refresh original sources when a task depends on a changing provider requirement or a dated tactic. Do not buy or collect more material just because this reference exists.
