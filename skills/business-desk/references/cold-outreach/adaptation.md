# Adaptation and examples

These are fictional illustrations, not client facts or ready to send campaigns. Replace placeholders and verify the premise, offer, identity and required footer. Specific offers must be approved and deliverable.

## Portable client brief

Collect only what the requested job needs: represented business, authorized sender, service and capacity, buyer role and geography, exclusions, verified reason for contact, known current process, usable proof, next step and reply owner. For launch, add provider, relevant jurisdiction, contact status and suppressions. For diagnosis, add results and the response window.

Label unknowns. Do not turn a quick rewrite into a lengthy intake interview. Resolve material launch requirements before sending.

## Website and software services

Choose one workflow the service actually improves. A quote form proves a public inquiry route exists. It does not establish response speed, lost revenue, CRM use or missed calls.

Assumptions: the form and business type are verified, and the provider can prepare the stated example.

Subject: After the quote request

Hi [Name],

I saw the quote request form on [Company]’s site. I build websites and inquiry systems for [business type].

If following up means moving details between email and a spreadsheet, I can show a simple workflow that keeps the request, owner and next action together.

Would you like a short example?

[Real sender and represented software business]
[Required contact and opt out details]

The conditional sentence avoids pretending to know the internal process. Adapt it when research establishes a different situation.

## Agency representing a recruiting client

Assumptions: a hiring page shows the role, the recruiter covers it and can provide the market note. Never invent available candidates.

Subject: [Role] hiring in [location]

Hi [Name],

Your careers page lists an opening for [role] in [location]. [Recruiting business] recruits for that type of position.

We can share a short note on the requirements candidates most often ask about before applying.

Would that help with this search?

[Authorized sender for the recruiting business]
[Required contact and opt out details]

The agency’s marketing results are not the recruiter’s proof. Keep client identity, evidence and replies separate.

## Agency selling its own service

Use the agency as the represented business. Preserve the actual offer, whether website design, media management, retention email, video production or another service. Do not turn it into booked meetings because a contributor teaches lead generation.

When no problem is verified, a direct qualification question can be more honest than invented personalization: “Are you planning to update [specific part of the website] this year?” Include enough service context to explain why you ask. Avoid asking strangers to diagnose their whole business with “What would you like your software to do better?”

## Limited proof

Use a concrete sample, documented method, scoped assessment or explanation of tradeoffs the team can provide. State the limits. Never disguise a speculative mockup as completed client work or promise a full free build without agreement.

An outside example remains that company’s work. A subcontractor’s results are not automatically the agency’s results. Use only permitted evidence with accurate attribution.

## Follow up example

Continue the software example only if the sender has the promised resource:

Hi [Name],

One part of the example may be useful: each inquiry gets an owner and a next action, so the team can see what still needs a response.

I can send the diagram here if you’d like to see it.

[Real signature and required details]

This adds a useful detail. It does not assert the prospect has missed inquiries or guilt them for silence.
